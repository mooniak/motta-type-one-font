Motta Type One is a heavy, rounded Sinhala display typeface designed by Damith Welikala and published by Mooniak. Motta (මොට්ට) means blunt: the letters are drawn with thick, soft-ended strokes, small counters and almost no contrast, so words read as dark, friendly shapes at large sizes. It suits headlines, posters, signage, packaging and titles.

The font covers the Sinhala alphabet with its vowel signs, the common rakar and yansaya forms, consonant + vowel-sign ligatures and touching conjuncts, plus digits and basic punctuation. It comes in a single Regular weight.

To contribute, see github.com/mooniak/motta-type-one-font.
