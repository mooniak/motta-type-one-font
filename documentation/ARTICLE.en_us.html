Motta Type One is a Sinhala headline typeface, Damith Welikala's 2020 revival of Motta Type: the letters Motagedara Wanigaratne designed for the Aththa newspaper in the 1960s. For nearly half a century they shaped the main headline on Aththa's front page, and in their day they were a very popular letter design.

The letters carry extra weight on their top and bottom edges, and their terminals are drawn from old Sinhala carving patterns. Several kinds of vowel signs are used depending on the letter, and ka, ta and na have their own i-sign. As digital typography spread in Sri Lanka, many metal-type designs fell out of use, Motta Type among them; this font brings it back.

The font covers the Sinhala alphabet with its vowel signs, rakar and yansaya forms, consonant + vowel-sign ligatures and touching conjuncts, plus digits and basic punctuation, in a single Regular weight.

To contribute, see github.com/mooniak/motta-type-one-font.
